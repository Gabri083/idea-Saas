Yotpo is probably the most recognized reviews app in the Shopify ecosystem, and it earned that position — strong integrations, a full marketing suite, and a product that genuinely works. Two things still push a lot of merchants toward comparing alternatives: pricing that climbs fast as order volume grows (the jump from entry-level Reviews to the full suite can go from under $100/month to several hundred once you're doing real volume), and Yotpo sunsetting some of its standalone Email and SMS products, which forced merchants who relied on that bundle to re-evaluate their whole stack anyway.

We're one of the alternatives below — Kelsira, our own product — and we're putting it first instead of pretending this is a neutral list with us buried at the bottom. Every option here gets a real, specific look, including where Yotpo still wins.

## Kelsira

**What it is:** Built around a structural rule rather than a policy — a business can't hide, filter, or edit a review before it goes live. An AI reads the actual text of each review and scores it on the facts described, separate from the star rating the customer happened to click.

**Why that's a different bet than Yotpo's:** Yotpo (and most competitors) compete on breadth — more marketing features, more integrations, more of your stack in one place. Kelsira competes on trust in the score itself. People are inconsistent raters — a customer having a bad day drops a 1-star that has nothing to do with your actual service, and it sits there dragging your average down forever. Kelsira's AI corrects for that in both directions, and it also surfaces recurring operational patterns across reviews (the same shipping carrier getting flagged by three different customers in a month, for instance) so you're fixing real problems, not just managing a number.

**Pricing:** Starter $29/month, Growth $79/month, Enterprise $199/month — with 30% off the first 6 months for the first 40 businesses that sign up.

**Honest limitation:** No loyalty program, no referrals, no SMS/email suite — if you specifically want Yotpo's all-in-one marketing stack, Kelsira isn't trying to replace that. We're solving a narrower, more specific problem: making the review score itself something neither side can quietly game.

## Judge.me

The most common landing spot for merchants leaving Yotpo purely over cost, and deservedly so. There's a genuine free plan (unlimited reviews, photo/video, SEO rich snippets), and the paid tier is a flat $15/month no matter your order volume — no scaling fees as you grow, which is the exact problem people are leaving Yotpo to avoid. That flat rate gets you AI-generated replies and translations, 130+ integrations, and all 16 widgets. What you give up versus Yotpo is the marketing-suite breadth — this is a reviews tool, not a full stack.

## Stamped.io

The closest like-for-like swap — reviews, a loyalty program (points, VIP tiers, referrals), and NPS in one platform, positioned specifically to compete with Yotpo on breadth at a lower price. 2026 pricing runs about $23/month at 200 orders up to $199/month at 5,000 orders, with a free tier under 50 orders and no overage fees for a breakout month. If Yotpo's all-in-one approach is what you actually want, just not at Yotpo's price, this is the direct comparison to run.

## Okendo

The pick if review *quality* and collection rate matter more to you than price. Okendo bundles reviews, loyalty, quizzes, surveys, and referrals, with AI-generated review summaries and searchable keywords layered on top of photo/video UGC — the visual review quality is genuinely a step up for apparel, beauty, and home goods brands specifically. Pricing runs $19/month (200 orders) up to $499/month (10,000 orders), with roughly 30% off if you bundle all four products instead of buying separately. One real constraint: Okendo is Shopify/Shopify Plus only — no WooCommerce, BigCommerce, or Wix support, so it's off the table if you're not on Shopify.

## WiserReview

The budget pick if you need multi-platform support beyond Shopify — it's the only option on this list that also covers WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Wix, and Squarespace. Pricing starts at $9/month (100 orders, unlimited reviews) up to $31/month for the Pro+AI tier (5,000 orders, AI review insights, custom integrations), plus a limited free plan to try it first. Requests go out over email, SMS, or WhatsApp, and there's a 14-day money-back guarantee with no lock-in — cancel or downgrade anytime.

## Migrating is less painful than it sounds

Every option above supports importing your existing reviews, and in practice the switch usually takes an afternoon, not a project. The real decision is what you're optimizing for next:

- **Cheapest with unlimited orders** → Judge.me.
- **Closest like-for-like swap to Yotpo, lower price** → Stamped.io.
- **Best collection quality/response rate, Shopify only** → Okendo.
- **Multi-platform, not just Shopify** → WiserReview.
- **You want the score itself to be something neither side can game, not just a fuller marketing stack** → that's what Kelsira is specifically built for.

Yotpo isn't a bad product — it's priced and packaged for a certain kind of store, and if that's not the one you're running, it's worth knowing what else is out there before your next renewal.
