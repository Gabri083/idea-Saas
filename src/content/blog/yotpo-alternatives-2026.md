Yotpo is probably the most recognized reviews app in the Shopify ecosystem, and it earned that position — strong integrations, a full marketing suite, and a product that genuinely works. So why are so many merchants actively comparing alternatives right now?

Two reasons show up constantly. First, pricing climbs fast as your order volume grows — the jump from the entry Reviews plan to the full suite (reviews, loyalty, referrals) can go from under $100/month to several hundred once you're doing real volume. Second, Yotpo has been sunsetting some of its standalone Email and SMS products, which has pushed merchants who relied on that bundle to re-evaluate their whole stack anyway. If you're already comparing tools, here's where people usually look.

## Judge.me

The most common landing spot for merchants leaving Yotpo purely over cost. Free plan available, and the paid tier stays cheap even with unlimited orders — no annual contract either. You give up some of Yotpo's marketing-suite breadth, but for a store that just wants solid review collection and display, it's hard to beat on price.

## Stamped.io

Closer to a like-for-like swap — reviews, loyalty, and NPS in one platform, positioned specifically as a Yotpo alternative for stores that want similar breadth without the same price tag at scale.

## Okendo

Worth a look if review *quality* and collection rate matter more to you than price — it's generally positioned as a stronger collection experience than Yotpo's default templates, at a lower price point than Yotpo's equivalent tier, though still a meaningful cost.

## WiserReview

The budget pick if you need multi-platform support beyond just Shopify (WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Squarespace), starting well under $20/month.

## Kelsira

Different angle entirely: instead of competing on "more reviews, more marketing features," we built Kelsira around making the review *score itself* trustworthy. Every review posts exactly as submitted — the business can't hide or filter what goes live — and an AI reads the actual text to score it on facts, not just the star the customer happened to click. A frustrated 1-star for something minor doesn't get to unfairly define your rating, and the score can't be quietly curated the way a business-controlled feed can be.

It's built for Shopify/WooCommerce specifically, includes embeddable widgets and bilingual support, and right now the first 40 businesses that sign up lock in 30% off their first 6 months.

## Migrating is less painful than it sounds

Every option above supports importing your existing reviews, and in practice the switch usually takes an afternoon, not a project. The bigger decision is what you're actually optimizing for going forward:

- **Cheapest with unlimited orders** → Judge.me.
- **Closest like-for-like swap** → Stamped.io.
- **Best collection/response rate** → Okendo.
- **Multi-platform, not just Shopify** → WiserReview.
- **You want the score itself to be something neither side can game** → that's the specific gap Kelsira is built to fill.

Yotpo isn't a bad product — it's just priced and packaged for a certain kind of store, and if that's not the one you're running, it's worth knowing what else is out there before your next renewal.
